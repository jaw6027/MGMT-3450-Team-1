01 / THE QUESTION BEHIND THE FLIGHT

What is your drone
experiencing?

Flight logs describe the journey. Structural measurements offer another perspective on what the aircraft experiences along the way.

Drone SHM explores how sensors on selected arms and near motor mounts can bring that perspective into one dashboard. SHM stands for structural health monitoring: observing how a structure responds over time.

A starting point for investigation and engineering review. Decisions still depend on calibration, context, and validated methods.

01 / STRUCTURE

STRAIN / TWO MONITORED ARMS

How does the arm respond?

Strain gauges measure local stretching or compression. Explore how readings change as the monitored structure is loaded.

A little more detail

The concept uses two strain gauges with HX711 interfaces. Microstrain describes local strain; it does not directly measure arm displacement.

02 / MOVEMENT

VIBRATION / TWO MEASUREMENT POINTS

What movement is present?

Acceleration measurements can be summarized to help a team explore vibration at two sensor locations.

A little more detail

The concept uses ADXL355 accelerometers. The dashboard expects g RMS, a summary of vibration amplitude over a measurement window, rather than raw sensor samples.

03 / TEMPERATURE

TEMPERATURE / TWO MOTOR LOCATIONS

How does temperature change?

Readings near motor mounts add thermal context to an evaluation, alongside the structural measurements.

A little more detail

The concept uses MCP9808 sensors reporting degrees Celsius. Mounting position matters: this is not necessarily the internal motor winding temperature.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before you
take a closer look.

Is this available as a finished product?

The linked project is an academic research prototype. Product availability, pricing, support, and installation have not been established. Use the demo to explore the concept and prepare questions for the project team.

Will the demo show readings from my drone?

No. Demo mode uses simulated data. Live mode needs a configured, trusted real sensor feed; choosing Live does not connect your aircraft automatically.

Can it tell me whether my drone is safe to fly?

The prototype does not provide a validated damage-detection or flightworthiness decision. Its visual colors and arm movement are illustrative, not certified warning thresholds.

Could the concept work with our aircraft?

Compatibility needs an engineering review of the airframe, sensor placement, power, communications, calibration, and data requirements. No universal compatibility is claimed.

What should we bring to an initial discussion?

Your use case, aircraft type, current inspection workflow, and the question you would like the measurements to help investigate. The brief below helps organize those points.
